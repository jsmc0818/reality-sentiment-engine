# Why I built Reality Sentiment Engine

JunSang (Dan) Cho

I want to understand when uncertainty creates an attractive entry into a strong
business, and when a falling share price reflects damage I should respect.

The research question is: **does today's price compensate me for today's
uncertainty?**

The dashboard compares three distinct bodies of evidence:

1. Market behavior: signed price pressure, relative performance and volume.
2. Business resilience: reported revenue, margins, cash generation and leverage.
3. Priced expectations: cash-flow scenarios and the growth needed to justify price.

My judgment belongs in the assumptions, counterarguments and invalidation
conditions. Data collection and calculation should make those judgments easier
to inspect and challenge. A high score is not a substitute for a thesis.

This project demonstrates a reproducible research process. It does not claim
validated alpha, successful market timing, or proven superiority over a fear-only
index. The default valuations are mechanical sensitivity cases. Independent,
company-specific forecasts and prospective evaluation remain research work.

For investing, the question is what price offers adequate compensation for risk.
For corporate finance and strategy, the related question is what growth and
investment returns justify deploying capital. Both require credible business
drivers, explicit assumptions and a willingness to revise a conclusion.

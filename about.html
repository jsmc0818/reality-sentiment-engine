ABOUT THE RESEARCH

Uncertainty deserves analysis.

I built this to understand when falling prices create an opportunity, and when they reveal a weakening business.

The question that started it

I am interested in owning strong businesses through uncertainty. A fearful market can create an attractive entry, but fear alone tells me little about the price I should pay. I wanted a tool that puts market behavior next to business evidence and the expectations embedded in a valuation.

Reality Sentiment Engine began with index-level panic and earnings-revision gauges. This research edition adds individual companies, operating evidence and a valuation lab. It asks: does today's price compensate me for today's uncertainty?

How I want to make decisions

  • Start with the business. Trace revenue, margins, capital needs and cash generation before drawing a conclusion from the share price.
  • Make assumptions visible. Separate reported results, analyst expectations and the scenarios used to estimate value.
  • Look for disconfirming evidence. Every company has a counterargument and a business condition that would force a reassessment.
  • Keep a dated record. Preserve observations so future results can be compared with what was actually available at the time.

What the dashboard proves today

It demonstrates a reproducible research workflow and makes valuation sensitivity inspectable. It does not establish an investment edge. Default scenarios are mechanical starting points, not independent company forecasts or price targets. The market-mood and resilience rules have not been validated as predictors.

What I am testing next

Does conditioning market pressure on business evidence and valuation improve accumulation decisions relative to fear alone? Evaluation must include failed signals, subsequent earnings deterioration, drawdowns, missed opportunities and the cost of waiting.

The earlier index backtest uses different inputs. Its results cannot validate the current stock model. An honest negative result would still improve this research.

Capital allocation, from either side

The same questions matter to an investor and a finance team: how much growth is credible, what investment does it require, and what return justifies committing capital? The scenario controls expose those relationships.
